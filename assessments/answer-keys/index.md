# Answer Keys

These files hold the keyed answers, scoring notes, and required elements for every graded self-assessment instrument. They live at the repository root, outside `docs/`, so MkDocs never builds them into the published site.

**Attempt first.** Open a key only after you have written and scored your own answers. A key read before an attempt tells you what the course wanted; it does not tell you whether you can produce it.

The rubrics in `assessments/rubrics/` carry instructor scoring notes and are public by design, because a learner needs the criteria before producing the artifact. The progress record is self-scored and has no key.

Each key states the correct option, explains why each distractor is wrong for multiple-choice items, and lists the required elements for short-answer and scenario items with acceptable alternatives. Tag lines such as `Tag: M04 · LO-10 · Bloom 3 · D2` identify the module, the learning outcome, the Bloom level, and the difficulty used in [Learning outcomes §4.3](../../docs/learning-outcomes.md#43-self-assessment-coverage).

These keys are not externally validated and are not proctored. See the [assessment validation report](../../docs/assessment-validation-report.md) for what was checked mechanically and what was not.
