# Knowledge Check — QZ-16

**Module 16 · 10 items · 15 points · about 20 minutes**

Answer without opening the project briefs. Items 4, 7, and 10 ask you to audit a statement or design.

---

**1. (1 point)** Which item is the best first deliverable of an ML security assessment?

A. A list of every attack technique the team knows  
B. A scope statement naming the system, decision, owner, versions, and non-goals  
C. A robustness curve for the current model  
D. A penetration-test report

---

**2. (1 point)** Match each asset to its most likely classification.

A. Published fee schedule  
B. Transaction feature snapshot  
C. Model signing key  
D. Aggregated merchant volume

Levels: public, internal, confidential, restricted.

---

**3. (2 points)** Explain why "we ran PGD and the attack failed" is not, by itself, a finding.

---

**4. (2 points)** A test plan contains this entry:

> T-11 — Check the model for adversarial robustness. Run PGD with a reasonable epsilon and inspect the results.

Name three defects that make this test unable to fail in a useful way, and rewrite it as a falsifiable test case.

---

**5. (1 point)** What is a zero-budget control, and what does a nonzero effect at zero budget usually indicate?

---

**6. (2 points)** A defense reduces attack success from 0.34 to 0.09, but the team also retuned the decision threshold from 0.50 to 0.35. Explain why the comparison is confounded and state two ways to fix the report.

---

**7. (2 points)** Audit this finding:

> **F-3 (High):** The model can be evaded. We changed several features and the score dropped. Fix the model.

List four missing elements of a usable finding.

---

**8. (1 point)** Why is a cheap detection control sometimes a better first remediation than an expensive retraining project?

---

**9. (1 point)** Which statement is the best residual-risk statement?

A. "All risks have been mitigated."  
B. "The system is secure after the fixes."  
C. "The tested segment evasion is now detected; an adaptive attacker inside the caps and a compromised vendor feed remain unaddressed."  
D. "Residual risk is low."

---

**10. (2 points)** An executive summary says "no critical issues were found." The findings table contains one Critical and two High items. What should happen before the report is released, and why does the mismatch matter?

---
